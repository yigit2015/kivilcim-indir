Today

  • A repeat that fits you

    Tasks repeat every day, a few times a week or on set days. You can also add a habit you want to quit; then your clean days are counted.

    Everywhere

  • Start small, grow it

    Counters, timers, sub-steps, a mini version and a goal that rises week by week; on a hard day a small step still counts.

    Everywhere

  • One tap or a swipe

    Complete a task with one tap or by swiping right, skip it with a joker by swiping left; the list is done in seconds.

    Everywhere

  • The Today orbit

    Kıvı stays in the middle and today's tasks slowly circle and float around it as bubbles. Tapping completes a task and holding opens its options; the ring around Kıvı shows the day's progress and closes completely when every task is done. If the tasks don't fit, the last bubble becomes “+N” and opens the list.

    Everywhere

  • The Up next card

    Only one card sits below the home screen at a time: a newly unlocked section, the reminder question, a ready chest, a letter, closing the day in the evening. The home screen never gets crowded.

    Everywhere

  • Today's steps

    The home screen header shows “You took 4,213 steps today”. Permission is asked once, after you tick your first task; if you decline, or the phone has no step counter, the line never appears.

    Coming with iPhone

  • Reminders on time

    Per task, a heads-up 15 minutes before and “did you do it?” 60 minutes after. Notifications can be normal, gentle or one daily summary; reminders never smother you.

    On phone

  • Location reminders

    Lets you know when you arrive at a place you chose; walk into the gym and your workout task comes to mind.

    On phone, in the background tooWeb: while the app is open

  • Export to calendar

    Export your tasks to the calendar app you use; the day's plan lives in one place.

    Everywhere

  • Check off with QR and NFC tags

    Print a QR tag and stick it on your fridge; scan it and the task is checked off. The same link can be written to an NFC tag too.

    With the phone appWith the web versionNFC writing: Android Chrome, beta

  • If–then

    A trigger like “after my coffee” ties a new habit to something you already do; remembering gets easier.

    Everywhere

  • A simpler list on bad days

    On a hard day the easiest tasks come first and the rest fold away. For newcomers, sections unlock day by day; not everything arrives on day one.

    Everywhere

Streak

  • 5 flame tiers

    As your streak grows, your flame changes color: a new tier on days 7, 30, 100 and 365. The color tells you how far a long streak has come.

    Everywhere

  • Protect your streak

    Freezes (up to 3), a streak shield, vacation mode and streak recovery; one missed day doesn't erase months of streak.

    Everywhere

  • Graduate a habit, start a new season

    You graduate a habit that has stuck. In a new season the counters reset; your history stays.

    Everywhere

  • Celebrations and day close

    A celebration when you reach a new tier and on every new streak day; in the evening, a summary that wraps up the day.

    Everywhere

  • Language without blame

    It doesn't scold you when a streak breaks; it shows how consistent you've been: “27 of the last 30 days, 90%”. You pick the coach's tone: Kind friend, Tough coach or Playful.

    Everywhere

Game

  • Sparks and bonuses

    10 sparks per task. Bonuses for a perfect day, early bird (05:00–09:00), star of the day (×3) and hard tasks; your effort becomes visible.

    Everywhere

  • Rewards and shop

    The Rewards tab holds the chest, the weekly challenge, adventure, world, season and luck; the shop sits below: task joker, power potion and streak shield. Everything is bought with sparks; nothing is bought with money.

    Everywhere

  • Cosmetics

    4 cosmetic themes (Synthwave, Glacier, Forest, Lava); confetti, flames and backgrounds; row, button and quote skins; avatars and costumes, name effects and frames. There's a deal of the day and item merging too.

    EverywhereCheck-in sound: web and desktop

  • Luck corner

    A lucky wheel, a mystery box and a spark piggy bank that pays interest; the sparks you save grow too.

    Everywhere

  • Chest strip and multiplier

    A chest every day on the 7-day chest strip; stop the marker at x1, x2, x4 or x10. Golden check-ins, spark showers and happy hour arrive as surprises too.

    Everywhere

  • Adventure

    A baby dragon, a weekly boss and the season path. One skill tree unlocks 6 powers across 3 tiers; the weekly challenge and real-life rewards you set yourself live here too.

    Everywhere

  • World

    A tower, an egg, a map, a habit garden, a city and habit cards; a small world that grows as you do your tasks.

    Everywhere

  • Badges and titles

    33 badges (4 hidden), 9 level titles from Spark to Supernova and titles you unlock with badges. At the end of the year, “Kıvılcım Wrapped” tells the story of your year.

    Everywhere

Know yourself

  • Charts, calendar and records

    Weekly chart, 12-month heatmap, calendar, forecast and records; see where you are and where you're heading at a glance.

    Everywhere

  • Monthly report card and habit strength

    Shows month by month how well each habit has stuck.

    Everywhere

  • Patterns and hard-day forecast

    Finds your best hours and tells you in advance which day might be hard; you plan around it.

    Everywhere

  • Spark economy

    A screen that shows where your sparks come from and where they go.

    Everywhere

  • Automaticity meter

    Measures with a few short questions how automatic a habit has become; you see whether it has stuck or still takes effort.

    Everywhere

Rituals

  • Journal

    Mood, intention, gratitude and a question-free “write quietly” page. Voice journaling depends on browser support: Chrome and Safari have it, Firefox doesn't.

    EverywhereVoice journal: depends on the browser

  • Just for you

    A letter to your future self, a quote book, a dream board and a birthday surprise; you won't forget why you started.

    Everywhere

  • Breathing and Pomodoro

    Breathing, focus rounds and countdowns; tap a breathing, journal or timed task and its tool opens, then the task checks off when you finish.

    Everywhere

  • Seasonal packs and starter quiz

    5 seasonal packs: New Year Resolutions, Winter Energy, Exam Season, Summer Shape and Back to School. The starter quiz suggests a start that fits you in five short questions.

    Everywhere

Share

  • Cards

    Streak card, weekly summary, report card story and achievement certificate; share your effort as an image.

    Everywhere

  • Together

    Friend duels by QR, pack sharing and joint challenges; with a code, no account needed.

    Everywhere

  • Send a cheer

    Support without scores: no race, no comparing, just courage.

    Everywhere

Family, backup and security

  • Family profiles and kid mode

    Every family member gets their own profile. In kid mode the shop, sharing and outside links are off; leaving it takes the parent gate.

    Everywhere

  • Lock

    PIN lock everywhere. Fingerprint works on Android too; Face ID comes with the iPhone version.

    PIN: everywhereFingerprint: AndroidFace ID: coming with iPhone

  • Back up and restore

    File, clipboard or CSV. A local backup is made automatically every day; on phone it can also be written to a cloud folder automatically.

    EverywhereCloud folder: on phone

  • Privacy center

    Shows what is stored and why each permission is asked for; you can delete everything in one place.

    Everywhere

  • Feedback

    Report a bug or send an idea from inside the app. Your task names and journal aren't added to the message.

    Everywhere

Appearance and accessibility

  • 6 theme options

    Automatic (follows your device), Night, AMOLED, Day, Aurora and Sakura; whichever is easiest on your eyes.

    Everywhere

  • Comfortable use

    Calm motion, color-blind friendly markers, one-handed use, a sky that follows the time and night softening.

    Everywhere

  • Text size and haptics

    3 text sizes on web and desktop; on phone the app follows your system text size. 4 haptic levels on phone.

  • Organizing

    Bulk edit and a task archive; tidying up gets easier when your list grows.

    Everywhere

  • Language

    English and Turkish; it follows your phone's language on first launch.

    Everywhere

    Support for iOS Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast comes with the iPhone version.
