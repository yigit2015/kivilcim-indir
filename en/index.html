Features

Habits, at their simplest.

All free. No account, no internet needed.

01

Tasks

One tick, a counter or a timer. Quitting a habit? Your clean days are counted.

02

Tap to start

Tap a breathing, focus or journal task; its tool opens and the task checks itself off when you finish.

03

Protect your streak

Freezes, a streak shield and vacation mode. One missed day won't erase months.

04

No-blame language

It never scolds you when a streak breaks. You pick the coach's tone.

05

Insights

A 12-month heatmap, monthly report card, your best hours and a hard-day forecast.

06

Close the day

Mood, energy, sleep and intention. Or a quiet page that asks nothing.

Insights

Get to know yourself.

See the pattern behind your streak: which hours suit you, which day is hard.

  • Weekly chart, calendar, forecast and records. See where you are and where you're heading at a glance.
  • Monthly report card and habit strength. Shows how settled each habit is.
  • Patterns. Finds your best hours; the hard-day forecast warns you ahead of time.
  • Spark economy screen. Where your sparks come from and where they go.
  • Automaticity meter. Measures how automatic a habit has become.
