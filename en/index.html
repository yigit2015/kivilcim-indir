How it works

  1. Add a task

    Write a task that repeats every day, a few times a week or on set days.

  2. Check it off

    Complete it with one tap, or swipe the task to the right.

  3. Earn sparks

    Every task brings 10 sparks. You spend sparks in the shop.

  4. Your streak grows

    Your flame grows a little every day. It changes color on days 7, 30, 100 and 365.

Protect your streak

  • Streak freeze

    Protects a day you miss. You can keep up to 3.

  • Streak shield

    Protects your streak for up to 3 missed days; bought with sparks in the shop.

  • Vacation mode

    Your streak waits while you're away; when you're back you pick up where you left off.

  • Streak recovery

    If your streak goes out, a recovery card appears on the home screen: complete the missing day and get your streak back.

  • Graduate a habit or start a new season

    Graduate a habit that has stuck. In a new season the counters reset; your history stays.

  • Small celebrations

    A celebration when you reach a new tier and on every new streak day; in the evening, a day close summary.

Earn sparks, spend them

Every task brings 10 sparks. Sparks can't be bought with money; you earn them only by doing tasks.

A coach that doesn't shame you

No blame when a streak breaks. Kıvılcım shows how consistent you've been: 27 of the last 30 days, 90%.

On a bad day the list gets simpler. If you like, notifications come gently or shrink to one daily summary. You also pick the coach's tone:

  • Kind friendIt might be a hard day. A small step still counts.
  • Tough coachNo excuses. Get up and finish that task.
  • PlayfulThe couch misses you a lot, but your tasks miss you more.

Family, backup and security

  • Family profiles and kid mode

    Every family member gets their own profile. In kid mode the shop, sharing and outside links are off; leaving it takes the parent gate.

  • Lock

    PIN lock everywhere. Fingerprint works on Android too; Face ID comes with the iPhone version.

  • Back up and restore

    File, clipboard or CSV. A local backup is made automatically every day; on phone it can also be written to a cloud folder automatically.

  • Privacy center

    Shows what is stored and why each permission is asked for. You can delete everything from here.

  • Feedback

    Report a bug or send an idea from inside the app. Your task names and journal aren't added to the message.

Install notes

Windows

If a SmartScreen warning appears, choose “More info” and then “Run anyway”.

Mac

The app is unsigned: on first launch, right-click and choose Open. On macOS 15 and later, System Settings → Privacy & Security → “Open Anyway”.

Linux

First make the AppImage file executable: chmod +x Kivilcim-linux.AppImage. Or install the .deb package.

Android

Allow installs from unknown sources when installing.

Updating doesn't delete your data. Earlier versions: All releases. What changed: Release notes.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Kıvılcım works without an account; your data stays on your phone.

Does the app cost money?

No. Kıvılcım has no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads; all themes and programs are free.

I'm switching phones. Will I lose my data?

Profile → “Back up and restore” → “Create and share a backup file” to make a file; on the new phone, tap “Choose a file” on the same screen. If you pick a cloud folder, backups are written automatically.

I want to delete all my data.

Profile → “Privacy center” → “Erase everything”. Type DELETE in the box to confirm. This can't be undone.
